The misterious Key

Emma lived in a small, quiet town. She worked in the local library and loved old
books. One rainy Tuesday, while she was organising the oldest section of the library, 
she found somethings unusual. A heavy, dusty book about  gardebing fell from the top 
shelf. When Emma picked it up, a small, silver key fell out from between the pages.

The key was cold and delicate. It loocked very old. There was a tiny number 
engraved on it 317. Emma was curious. She knew all the keys in the library, but this 
one was different. It didnt fit any door, cabinet, or drawer in the building.

That afternoon, she showed the key to her colleague, Mr Higgins. He had worked at 
the library for forty years. He put on his glasses and examineted the key carefully.

"Ah" he said slowly. "This isnt a library key. Many,  many  years ago, before this was 
a library, the building was a hotel. Room 317 was one of the best rooms. This must
be the key to that room."

"But where is Room 317 now?" Emma asked. The library  only had one floor and a 
basement.

Mr. Higgins pointed to north wall, which was now covered  with bookshelves
fillled with travel guides. "Somewhere behind there, I suppose. When they changed
the hotel into a library fifty years ago, they built new wall. Room 317 was closed
forever."

Emma couldnt stop thinking about the old key. Why was it hidden in a book? What
was behind that wall? That night, she had a dream about the old hotel. She saw a 
lonely traveller locking the door of Room 317 and hiding the key in a book.

The next day, Emma decided to investigate. After the library closed, she carefully 
moved the travel guides from the shelves against the north wall. Behind them, she 
found nothing special-yust and old, plain wall. She felt disappointed.

But then, her hand touched a small, almost invisible crack in the wall. It was shapened.
like a door! Her heart beat fast. She pushed the pushed the wall gently. Nothing happened.
Then, she remembered the key. 

Whith a slightly shaking hand, Emma tried to inset the silver key into the crack. It fit
perfecty. She turned the key. There  was a solt click.

A small  section of the wall, just big enough for a person, swung inwards. It was dark
inside. Emma used her phone as a flashlight ans stepped into the room.

Room. 317 was frozen in time. There was a dusty bed, an old chair, and a small desk.
On the desk, there was a leather journal and a faded photograph of a young couple.
Emma open the journal. The first page said: "To whoever finds this. My love and I
met in  this room. We had to keep it a secret. I am leaving this key so that our happy
place is never forgotten."

Emma smailed. She didnt find treasure, but she found a beatifull story. She took the
photo and the journal,close the secret door, and put the key in her pocket.

The next day, she framed the old photo and put it on the library board with
a note: "Every place has a story. Whats yours?"

Wow, Emma always looks at the travel guide section abs smalies. She knows a 
wonderful secret is hiding just behind them.
