The Lost Necklace

It was the day of Annas best friends  wedding. Anna was excited but also very
nervous. She had an inportant job as a bridesmaid, and she wanted everything to
be perfect.

That morning, Anna opened her jewellery box to put on her special neckalace. It was 
a simple silver necklace with a small blue stone, a gift from her granmother. But the 
necklace was not there! Annas heart started beating fast. She looked everywhere:
under the bed, on the dressing table, in the bathroom. Nothing.

"Think, Anna, think!" she said to herself. She had worn the necklace last week to a 
dinner. Did she leave it at the restaurant? She called the restaurant, but the said no.

She felt terrible. That necklece was her "something old" and "something blue" for 
the wedding. It brought her good luck. Without it, she felt something was missing.

Just as she was about to give up, her litle brother,Leo, walked into her room.
"Whats wrong?" he asked. Anna explained about the lost necklace. Leo thought for 
a moment. "Did you check the pocket of your grey coat? You wore it to the dinner."

Anna ran to her wardrobe. She put her hand in the pocket of the grey coat... and felt 
something cold and metallic. It was the necklace! She had taken it off in the taxi 
brecause the clasp flet loose, and she had completely forgotten about it.

"Leo, you are a genious!" she shouted, hugging her brother. She put the necklace on,
and the clasp was fine. It looked beautiful with her dress.

At the wadding, everything was perfect. The bride was happy, the sun was shinning,
and Anna  felt calm and lucky. During the party, she danced with Leo and thacked
him again.

That day, Anna learned two things: always check your coat pockets, and sometimes,
the best help comes from the youngest person in the family.
